Project Defense / Viva Note: The INA219 sensor in this prototype measures DC electrical load (up to 26V / 3.2A) across a precision shunt resistor. For prototype demonstrations, a low-voltage DC light / LED load is safely energized via the Relay. In a full industrial deployment measuring 230V AC mains energy, an isolated non-invasive Current Transformer (CT) sensor or PZEM-004T module with galvanic optocoupler isolation is required for electrical safety.
